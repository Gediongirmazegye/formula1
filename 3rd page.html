Formula 1 Technical & Sponsorship Glossary

World Drivers' Championship (WDC)
The annual FIA championship awarded to the highest-scoring Formula 1 driver across the Grand Prix calendar.
Title Sponsor
The primary commercial partner integrated directly into a constructor's official FIA entry name (e.g., Oracle Red Bull Racing, Scuderia Ferrari HP, McLaren Mastercard F1 Team, Audi Revolut F1 Team).
Chassis vs. Power Unit
In Formula 1, each constructor designs and builds its own aerodynamic chassis (such as the Ferrari SF-26 or McLaren MCL40), while either manufacturing its own factory power unit or purchasing a customer hybrid engine from an OEM supplier (Ferrari, Mercedes-AMG, Red Bull Ford, Honda, or Audi).
