About Formula One

Formula One, commonly known as F1, is a global motorsport championship. Teams design and build advanced racing cars, while drivers compete in a series of Grand Prix events held at circuits around the world.

The championship is organized by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). Each season includes a Drivers' Championship and a Constructors' Championship.
